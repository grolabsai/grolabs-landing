Standards built on the scale

  • Section padding (vertical): py-2xl md:py-3xl — 80 px mobile, 160 px desktop. Always on the outer <section>, never on the inner max-w-page div.
  • Section title → content gap: mb-lg md:mb-[48px] — 24 px mobile, 48 px desktop. On every section <h2>.
  • Horizontal page padding: px-margin-mobile md:px-margin-desktop — 16 px / 48 px. Always paired with inner max-w-page mx-auto.
  • Footer top padding: pt-2xl md:pt-[120px] — extra breathing room (3× the bottom).

Layout

Section-padding pattern

Padding lives on the outer <section>, the inner div is just max-w-page mx-auto. The inverted pattern (padding on the inner div) causes content to start further-right than the header on viewports wider than 1440 px.

<!-- ✅ Right -->
<section class="py-2xl md:py-3xl px-margin-mobile md:px-margin-desktop bg-canvas">
  <div class="max-w-page mx-auto">
    ...
  </div>
</section>

<!-- ❌ Wrong -->
<section class="py-2xl bg-canvas">
  <div class="max-w-page mx-auto px-margin-mobile md:px-margin-desktop">
    ...
  </div>
</section>

Sections alternate between the lighter canvas, the deeper canvas, and the yellow accent so the eye reads them as distinct blocks.

Voice rules

Typography decisions that affect copy

  • Marker headlines are the brand voice. Hero headlines and every section <h2> are Permanent Marker, all caps, white.
  • Eyebrows = monospace caps in yellow. Every section gets one above its H2. font-mono tracking-[0.2em] text-xs uppercase text-primary mb-4 block.
  • Stats use Marker; their explanation uses Hanken. Mixing voices in one card is intentional — the stat is the punchline, the body is the rationale.
  • Caveat is for secondary handwritten lines. Different texture than the marker, used sparingly. Registered for future use.
  • Black text is allowed only on yellow surfaces. Title + eyebrow on the Grow-a-Company section is the canonical use case.
