Most small e-commerce stores quietly bleed revenue in five places: internal search, product
description SEO, checkout friction, returns from mismatched expectations, and — increasingly —
answer engines bypassing them entirely. None of these are new problems, but the tooling to solve
them used to require an enterprise budget.

GroLabs exists to close that gap. We're shipping a small, opinionated stack that diagnoses where
your funnel is leaking, applies a targeted fix, and verifies the result within hours — not
quarters. It's built on the assumption that the difference between scaling and stagnating is
usually two or three friction points, not a wholesale platform rewrite.

This journal will follow the work. Expect posts on:

- How we measure funnel leakage when GA4 alone isn't enough
- The economics of internal site search at small catalog sizes
- Practical AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) for product pages
- Build notes on the GroLabs platform itself — what we ship, what we ripped out

If you're a small store operator who wants to recover lost revenue without paying for more
traffic, the rest of this site is for you. If you want the behind-the-scenes — that's here.
